Download Shree Lipi Marathi Fonts ( श्री देव लिपि )

ShreeLipi is a None-Unicode Marathi Devanagari typeface font. It is also known as Marathi Legacy Fonts. These Fonts uses English characters to produce Marathi script. You will not be able to see Marathi contents written in this fonts properly if you don’t have Shree Lipi fonts installed on your system.

These are old but most popular Marathi fonts that’s why they are called legacy fonts. Generally, these kinds of fonts are used widely to write marathi in legacy (Shree Dev Lipi) format.

Here on Hindityping.info we provide you a large Collection of Shree Lipi Regular, Designer (Fancy) Stylish Marathi Fonts including widely used Shree Dev Lipi 0706, 0708, 0714. Which you can easily download and use it in word documents as well as in Adobe photoshop & Pixel Labs like softwares. You can also download all Marathi ShreeLipi fonts zip file from bottom of the page.
